2. zxcvbn (algorithm, not code)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The strength estimator is inspired by zxcvbn by Dropbox, Inc.
(https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2012-2016
Dan Wheeler and Dropbox, Inc.). It re-implements the published approach
(pattern matching, minimum-guesses decomposition, score thresholds and
feedback wording) independently in C#; it is not a port and does not embed
zxcvbn's frequency lists, so its numbers differ from zxcvbn's.

The English word list (Resources/english-words.txt) was compiled for this
package from general knowledge of common English vocabulary.
